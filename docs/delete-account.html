Delete your Playtest Tracker account

In the app (fastest)

Open Profile, then Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Everything is removed immediately.

Without the app

Email the address you signed up with to the support address listed on the store page, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your account and email, every game you created, and all builds, sessions, events, reports, retests and event definitions under them. Nothing is kept.

Testers who played a game send only a random install ID, not a name or email. Their data is deleted with the developer's game.

Host this file at a public URL and add that URL to the Play Console data-safety form. The support address must be filled in before publishing.
